Clear Signposting Phrases

Signposts guide the examiner smoothly through your thought process during the 1-minute response.

1. Stance & Introduction
    stroke="currentColor"
  • "When it comes to [topic], I firmly believe that..."
  • "Speaking personally, I would definitely advocate for..."
  • "To address this question directly, I am inclined to think that..."
2. First Supporting Argument
  • "First and foremost, the key advantage is that..."
  • "To begin with, one major factor to consider is..."
  • "The primary reason behind this phenomenon is that..."
3. Second Supporting Argument / Shift
  • "Equally important, another aspect worth pointing out is..."
  • "On top of that, we should also look at..."
  • "On the flip side, looking at it from another angle..."
4. Smooth Conclusion
  • "In a nutshell, taking all these factors into account..."
  • "All in all, for the reasons mentioned above..."

Using Concrete Examples

To score Band 5+ in Content, avoid vague generalizations. Use specific Hong Kong contexts, personal experiences, or news trends!

Hong Kong Local Context Templates
  • "For instance, in densely populated districts like Mong Kok or Sham Shui Po..."
  • "Take public transit like the MTR or KMB as a concrete example..."
  • "A typical scenario in Hong Kong secondary schools is..."
Personal Observation & Social Media Examples
  • "Speaking from my own experience as a Form 6 student..."
  • "I frequently observe teenagers on Instagram and YouTube..."
  • "My friends and classmates often encounter this when..."
Level Classification Quick Reference
  • Easy (Q1 - Q3): Personal preferences, habits, immediate experiences.
  • Medium (Q4 - Q6): Reasons, comparisons, opinions on social or school trends.
  • Challenging (Q7 - Q8): Abstract evaluation, policy suggestions, societal impact, philosophy.
