5. Response Structure

Recommended 1-Minute Response Breakdown:

0s 5s 30s 55s 60s |---------|------------------------|------------------------|--------| Phase 1 Phase 2 Phase 3 Phase 4 Stance Point 1 + Example Point 2 + Example Conclusion

Phase 1: Rephrase Question & State Stance (0s – 5s)

  • Goal: Directly address the prompt and state your position immediately.
  • Key Phrases: "To answer this directly, I firmly believe that...", "Speaking personally, I would definitely advocate for..."

Phase 2: 1st Main Point + Concrete Example (5s – 30s)

  • Goal: Lead with your strongest main argument and a solid concrete example.
  • Key Phrases: "First and foremost, the primary reason is...", "For instance, in densely populated areas like Mong Kok..."

Phase 3: 2nd Main Point + Concrete Example (30s – 55s)

  • Goal: Introduce a distinct second angle (a new benefit, counter-perspective, or secondary point).
  • Key Phrases: "Equally important, another aspect worth highlighting is...", "On top of that, from a broader perspective..."

Phase 4: Brief Conclusion (55s – 60s)

  • Goal: Wrap up cleanly in a single sentence.
  • Key Phrases: "All in all, taking all these factors into account,...", "In a nutshell, for the reasons mentioned above,..."

6. Vocab

Useful high-band vocabulary and signpost expressions for your response:

  • Advocate: To publicly support or recommend a policy or course of action.
  • Dramatically: By a striking amount; significantly.
  • Fundamental: Forming a necessary base or core; of central importance.
  • Perspective: A particular attitude towards or way of regarding something; a point of view.
